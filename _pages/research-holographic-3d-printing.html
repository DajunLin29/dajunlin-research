Single-shot Holographic 3D Printing

I use inverse-designed holographic phase masks to form entire 3D light fields in a single exposure. This work combines optical design, grayscale lithography, and engineered resins for hollow volumetric structures and high-aspect-ratio microfabrication.

Volumetric additive manufacturing

Single-exposure holographic 3D printing via inverse-designed phase masks

Science Advances · 2026

I develop single-shot holographic printing of millimeter-scale, hollow 3D structures without stitching. The approach combines inverse-designed phase masks, nanofabrication, and engineered photopolymer resins to form a volumetric light dose in one exposure.

High-aspect-ratio microfabrication

Single-exposure holographic lithography of ultra-high aspect-ratio microstructures

Nature Communications · 2026

I develop volumetric photolithography to overcome the shallow depth of conventional photolithography and the low throughput of serial point writing. This work creates high-aspect-ratio microstructures for mechanical and fluidic applications.
