Differentiable Flat Lenses

I develop differentiable ray-tracing and wave-propagation models for flat and hybrid refractive–diffractive lenses. My work connects inverse design with nanofabrication, lens assembly, and neural reconstruction for compact wide-field cameras and AR/VR optics.

Ongoing research

I build end-to-end differentiable models that combine ray tracing and wave propagation for refractive–diffractive lens design. The work connects optical optimization with neural image reconstruction for wide-angle, achromatic imaging.
