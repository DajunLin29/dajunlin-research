Optical communication

CNN-assisted 768-ary free-space optical communication

Optics Express · 2021

I developed a convolutional-neural-network decoder for a Laguerre–Gaussian-mode shift-keying free-space optical communication system. The system supports robust color-image transmission through an experimental optical link.

Contributions & technical details

M.Eng. thesis & Research Assistant · Min Gu group · 2018–2021

  • Implemented an SLM-based setup for Laguerre–Gaussian beams and turbulence generation.
  • Built automated experimental data collection and a CNN-based decoder.
  • Demonstrated a 10-m, 768-ary system with 95% accuracy and latency below 2.7 ms.
