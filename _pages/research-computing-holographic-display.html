Computing & Holographic Display

I explore how structured light can process and carry information. Projects include diffractive neural-network chips, orbital-angular-momentum holography, CNN-assisted optical communication, and 3D displays based on perovskite nanocrystals in glass.

Diffraction-free orbital angular momentum holography

Optics Express · 2025

This work explores diffraction-free orbital angular momentum holography. An extended-depth-of-focus flat lens supports OAM-multiplexed holographic imaging over a larger depth range than a single target plane.

Compact eternal diffractive neural network chip for extreme environments

Communications Engineering · 2024 · Co-first author

A compact diffractive neural network performs optical inference using two patterned surfaces on a quartz substrate. My work spans differentiable network design, experimental organization, and optical testing for handwritten digit recognition.

Optical communication

CNN-assisted 768-ary free-space optical communication

Optics Express · 2021

I developed a convolutional-neural-network decoder for a Laguerre–Gaussian-mode shift-keying free-space optical communication system. The system supports robust color-image transmission through an experimental optical link.

Paper ↗
Contributions & technical details

M.Eng. thesis & Research Assistant · Min Gu group · 2018–2021

  • Implemented an SLM-based setup for Laguerre–Gaussian beams and turbulence generation.
  • Built automated experimental data collection and a CNN-based decoder.
  • Demonstrated a 10-m, 768-ary system with 95% accuracy and latency below 2.7 ms.
